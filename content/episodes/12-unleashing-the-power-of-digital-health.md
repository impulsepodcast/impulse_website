## Episode notes

Daniel Kraft is a surprising and admirable figure in the digital health landscape.

Originally trained as a physician in internal medicine and pediatrics, he then distinguished himself by his research work focusing on regenerative medicine, while at the same time initiating several entrepreneurial projects in the medtech space. [RegenMed Systems](https://www.regenmedsystems.com/) is one of them, a company that he founded and which led him to create a new type of medical device to facilitate bone marrow harvest procedures. [IntelliMedicine](https://www.intellimedicine.com/) is another one, where he aims to create a system enabling patients to 3D print their own medication at home, avoiding the intake of multiple pills on a daily basis. Aside from his own entrepreneurial endeavours, he advises several Fortune-50 and digital health-related startups, and is on the board of [Healthy.io](https://healthy.io/eu/).

As one of the most renowned ambassadors of digital health, he is driven by the conviction that innovation in this space can only result from the synergies of disciplines and technologies that may initially be totally distant from the medical sphere. Through his activities at the [Singularity University](https://www.su.org/) or through the program [Exponential Medicine](https://danielkraftmd.net/exponential-medicine) that he initiated, he brings together brilliant minds from a broad range of specialties who come together to explore convergent and rapidly developing technologies, to develop relevant applications in healthcare.

Often called upon to speak to the future of health, medicine and technology, he has already given multiple [TED and TEDMED Talks](https://danielkraftmd.net/ted-talks), and delivered keynotes to a wide array of organizations on those subjects.

In this episode of Impulse, we talked with Daniel about his early days in the healthcare world, why he decided to study medicine and orient himself on this path, his perspective on the importance of collaboration and boldness when it comes to creating new digital health solutions, what the future looks like from his lens, but also how he manages to stay on top of things while keeping a healthy lifestyle!
